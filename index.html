About Us

Crafting Quality Since 1999

A legacy of precision manufacturing and unwavering commitment to quality

Established as a Proprietorship firm in 1999, Gyanti Industries has grown into a leading manufacturer of premium aluminium perfume bottle caps and collars. Based in Kalyan, Thane, Maharashtra, we serve clients across India and beyond.

With over 25 years of industry expertise, we combine traditional craftsmanship with modern manufacturing techniques to deliver packaging components that meet the highest standards of quality and aesthetics.
